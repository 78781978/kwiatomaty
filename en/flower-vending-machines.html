FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a flower vending machine cost?

8-chamber flower machines start at PLN 30,000 net. The price depends on the model, equipment and configuration - I'll prepare an exact quote after a short conversation about your project.

How long does implementation take?

Usually 2-3 months - from placing the factory order, through shipping and customs clearance, to installation and launch at your location.

Who restocks the flowers in the machine?

Usually the location owner. As part of the training, I show how to restock the chambers quickly and efficiently.

Do you offer warranty and service?

The machines come directly from the factory in China I work with under a distribution agreement. I also provide warranty and service contact on the manufacturer's side.

Do you only deal with flower machines?

No - flower machines are the starting point of the offer, which also covers other vending machines. Write to me about the project you're planning, and I'll confirm the scope of cooperation.

Ready for your own flower vending machine?

Write to me - I'll prepare a free quote tailored to your idea.
