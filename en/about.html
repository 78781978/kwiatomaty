About

First-hand experience behind every flower machine

Before I offer you a flower vending machine, I use one myself. I don't sell something I don't know inside out.

My story

Why I work with flower vending machines

It started with my own business - I imported my first flower vending machine from a factory in China to test the machine in my own operation. Today I'm buying another machine from that same factory and have a signed distribution agreement with them - an ongoing partnership with a proven manufacturer, not casual brokering of one-off orders.

Along the way I learned the whole process inside out: choosing a model, negotiating with the factory, shipping, customs clearance, configuration and the first launch. For clients who want their own flower vending machine or other vending machine, I offer full-service support for that same process - without the mistakes I had to solve myself the first time.

Partnership with the factory

Cooperation built on trust and shared growth

My 10-day visit to the factory in China wasn't just about learning the production processes and technology - above all, it was about building lasting business relationships.

Meeting the factory owners in person let me build a friendly partnership based on mutual trust, respect and a shared vision for growth.

I especially value their openness to my suggestions and their willingness to introduce improvements and solutions tailored to the needs of the European market.

I don't just want to sell machines. I want to have a real impact on their quality, functionality and development.

Because behind the VERO Vending Machines brand stand not just the machines, but also people, relationships and shared ambitions.
